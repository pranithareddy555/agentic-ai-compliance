Internal AML/KYC Operating Policy (Draft)

New retail customers may open an account immediately, and identity verification can be completed within 90 days after onboarding if operations teams are backlogged.

Transactions above $15,000 are reviewed monthly by the compliance team, unless a manual escalation is separately raised.

Sanctions screening is performed once a year for all customers. PEP screening is also performed annually unless a regulator specifically asks for more frequent checks.

Customer due diligence files may be deleted after 12 months to reduce storage costs.

Staff should use judgment when deciding whether enhanced due diligence is necessary for high-value customers.
