Internal AML/KYC Operating Policy

Customer identity must be verified before account activation, except for explicitly approved low-risk exceptions with documented controls.

Risk profiling is mandatory at onboarding and refreshed when customer activity or profile changes indicate elevated risk.

Transactions are monitored using daily rules and escalated immediately when suspicious patterns are detected.

Sanctions and PEP screening is performed at onboarding and on an ongoing basis when watchlists or customer circumstances change.

Due diligence and monitoring records are retained in accordance with legal and regulatory obligations.
