# Mock FINRA / AML / KYC Guidelines

## Customer Identification Program
Financial institutions must collect and verify customer identity information before allowing ongoing account activity for customers that present material risk. Verification controls should be risk-based and documented.

## Customer Due Diligence
Institutions should maintain a risk profile for each customer, understand the nature and purpose of the relationship, and apply enhanced due diligence where risk indicators are present.

## Ongoing Monitoring
Transaction monitoring should be timely, risk-sensitive, and capable of escalating suspicious activity for investigation without unreasonable delay.

## Record Retention
AML, KYC, and due diligence records must be retained according to the institution's legal retention obligations. Short retention periods that impair auditability are not acceptable.

## Sanctions and PEP Screening
Institutions should perform sanctions and politically exposed person screening at onboarding and during the life of the customer relationship when risk or profile changes occur.
