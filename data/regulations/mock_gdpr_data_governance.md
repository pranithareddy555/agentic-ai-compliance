# Mock GDPR / Data Governance Controls

## Data Minimization
Only data necessary for a legitimate regulatory and business purpose should be collected and retained.

## Purpose Limitation
Personal data must be processed only for clearly defined and documented purposes.

## Access Governance
Access to regulated personal data should be role-based, auditable, and limited to authorized users.

## Retention and Deletion
Retention periods should be documented, justified, and aligned with legal obligations. Deletion should not conflict with mandatory regulatory retention requirements.
